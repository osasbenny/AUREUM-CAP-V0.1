# CAP-001 Owned-Destination Opportunity

## Opportunity statement

Zoey’s public presence already distributes expertise, personality, services, and social proof across platform surfaces. The opportunity for a Personal Brand HQ is to make those signals easier to understand and act on in one destination that she can control and expand.

This is an opportunity hypothesis, not a claim that Zoey lacks a website. The first artifact must demonstrate the value of a centralized destination without criticizing the platforms that currently bring attention to her.

## What the HQ adds

The HQ can create a clear sequence for a visitor who discovers Zoey through a post, search result, recommendation, or social profile:

1. Understand who Zoey is.
2. Understand which problems she helps with.
3. See the service categories supported by her public profile.
4. Read carefully sourced proof or recommendations.
5. Explore her ideas and content themes.
6. Follow her verified social channels.
7. Choose a verified next step such as contact, consultation, training, or mentorship.

## Product concept

The build should feel like a personal authority hub rather than a corporate brochure. It should combine:

- Editorial hero and positioning.
- A concise service architecture.
- Evidence-led proof.
- Selected ideas or content themes linked back to originals.
- Social ecosystem links.
- A clear, low-friction work-with-Zoey path.

## Conversion thesis

The demo should make the following flow visible without explaining it as sales copy:

> **Discovery → identity → trust → service fit → action.**

The site becomes the place where social attention can become understanding and a qualified next step.

## First-build content decisions

Use the profile’s service categories and voice as the foundation. Avoid hard-coded audience metrics, unsupported case studies, and unverified booking/contact details. Prefer “Selected perspectives” or “What Zoey helps with” over invented proof walls when evidence is limited.

The strongest first CTA should be derived from the current public profile. The phrase “DM RESULTS” is publicly visible on the LinkedIn profile, but an external contact action should not be wired until its current status and intended use are confirmed.
