# CAP-001 Claims Register

## Safe to publish, with source attribution where appropriate

- Zoey Vincent Vevakpor presents herself publicly as a Brand Strategist and social-media professional.
- Her public profile describes social-media management, ghostwriting/content writing, profile optimization or audits, brand strategy, content strategy, training, consultations, and mentorship.
- Her public content uses a conversational, humorous, direct, and encouraging communication style.
- Her public Instagram profile presents a purple-heart visual cue and combines professional, creator, and lifestyle content.
- Zoey’s public presence includes LinkedIn and Instagram channels that can serve as verified social destinations.

## Use cautiously and attribute to the source

- “Helped over 1,300 brands.” This is a self-reported LinkedIn profile claim.
- “Generated over $100,000 in revenue.” This is a self-reported LinkedIn profile claim.
- Favikon rankings or recognitions. The profile states them, but independent verification and current date context are required.
- Public recommendations. Use only after confirming the recommendation source and whether displaying the text is appropriate.
- Ziitech Hub founder association. A public event post supports the association, but the current role and website relationship should be checked before using it as a central brand element.

## Rejected for the first build unless independently verified

- Any exact current follower or audience number. Public sources displayed inconsistent LinkedIn counts.
- Any statement that Zoey has no website or owned digital destination.
- Any client list, case-study result, revenue result, award, ranking, or credential not supported by a direct current source.
- Any booking URL, WhatsApp number, email address, or contact route not confirmed as current and intended for public contact.
- Any claim that she exclusively serves B2B technology founders.

## Build rule

If a claim is not in the safe-to-publish section, it must either be omitted, visibly attributed as a self-reported statement, or held for owner review. The page must never convert uncertainty into marketing fact.
