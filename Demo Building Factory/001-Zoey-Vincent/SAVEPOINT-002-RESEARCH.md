## Opportunity finding

The defensible opportunity is not that Zoey lacks a website. That was not conclusively established and must not be stated publicly. The opportunity is to organize her existing public positioning, services, proof signals, educational content, and social distribution into a single owned destination where visitors can understand her offer and choose a next step.

The first HQ should demonstrate the path:

> **Social discovery → clear positioning → proof → service fit → ideas → contact or booking.**
